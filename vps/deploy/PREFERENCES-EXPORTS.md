# Browser preferences and research exports (3.10.0)

Homepage preferences are versioned in localStorage under `zohurnews.home.v1`: source order and visibility, filters, language, city, refresh toggle and accordion state. New sources append to saved order. Reset restores defaults. Research preferences use `zohurnews.research.v1`: view, applied filters, time range, media source subset and up to four selected article IDs; records are reloaded from the database. Preferences belong to this browser and origin, do not sync across devices, and disappear when site data is cleared. Blocked storage does not prevent use.

The research toolbar exports the current archive/filter set, selected comparison or loaded media comparison to actual XLSX and PDF. Dossiers have their own export buttons. Archive exports include at most 5,000 records for XLSX or 200 for PDF, independent of visible pagination. Files disclose matched/returned totals and truncation; narrow filters for complete exports. Media/day PDF tables disclose their 200-row cap. Excel sheets preserve full aggregates. Reports use stored records and explicit evidence, not generated judgments.

GET `/api/document-export` accepts `format=pdf|xlsx`, `kind=archive|media|selected|dossier`, the normal archive filters, or canonical comma-separated `ids` for selected/dossier. Selection is explicit and independent of archive filters. One export is rendered at a time (429 and Retry-After for contention). Renderer has a 25-second timeout and 32 MiB response bound. Strings in XLSX remain strings (including formula prefixes); PDF content is escaped and external fetching denied. DejaVu fonts provide Persian shaping. Writable font cache lives under /tmp/zohurnews-cache; research container tmpfs is 128 MiB.

A derived SQLite index `r_source_published(source_id,published_at,id)` speeds up source comparisons. Earliest articles are looked up at the already aggregated minimum timestamp. No archive content is rewritten.
